    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in New Iberia, LA

    How Finasteride + Minoxidil Works in New Iberia

    Finasteride blocks type 2 5-alpha reductase, the enzyme that turns testosterone into DHT. With less DHT around, the follicles on the crown and temples stop shrinking as quickly, and many hold their size for years instead of fading out. Minoxidil adds something different. It keeps hairs in their growing phase longer, so they come in thicker.

    Who This Combination Is For

    It is mainly for adult men with male pattern hair loss, such as a receding hairline or thinning crown. Early treatment keeps the most hair, since long-bald spots seldom respond. Postmenopausal women sometimes use finasteride off-label. It is never used in women who might become pregnant.

    Dosing and Daily Schedule in New Iberia

    Oral finasteride is FDA-approved at 1 mg once a day for male pattern hair loss. Topical minoxidil is applied to a dry scalp once or twice daily, while low-dose oral minoxidil, often 1.25 to 2.5 mg, is an off-label daily pill some men prefer to avoid the mess. One spray daily also works. The combination spray is applied to the scalp each day.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Expect some shedding in months one and two. That early shed usually comes from minoxidil pushing resting hairs out so new growing hairs can take their place, and it typically settles by month three. Around month six, density starts to improve. Long-term trials showed most men held their hair for years. Give it a year.

    Side Effects of Finasteride + Minoxidil in New Iberia

    Finasteride may lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in about one to two percent of men. These usually resolve after stopping, and rare mood changes, including low mood, should be reported to your provider right away. Topical minoxidil can itch or flake. The oral form sometimes causes fluid retention or a quicker pulse. The spray may reduce systemic side effects.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, heart conditions, blood pressure, mood history and sexual health. Expect questions about your medication list and family plans, because both can shape the choice. PSA results drop by about half. Women who could become pregnant should not handle crushed or broken tablets.

    How It Compares to Other Options in New Iberia

Dutasteride + minoxidil is a stronger, off-label option, usually considered after a year on finasteride without enough change in the photos. Minoxidil + GHK-Cu is topical-only.
